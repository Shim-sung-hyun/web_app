Draw ONE object and its shadow on a pure white background (white paper, not cream). SHADOW (important): the sun is high in the upper LEFT, behind the viewer (do not draw the sun), so the object's one shadow falls ONLY on its RIGHT side. The shadow starts at the bottom-right corner of the object, exactly where it touches the ground, and stretches away toward the UPPER RIGHT of the picture, like a clock hand pointing at 2 o'clock, a little longer than the object is tall. The LEFT side and the FRONT (bottom edge) of the object have NO shadow at all: the white paper there touches the outline directly. The shadow is a soft, smooth, flat, neutral cool-grey tone (not green, not brown), like a light grey watercolour wash: no outline, no hatching lines, no texture. It is darker near the object and lighter farther away. No grey on the paper anywhere else.
