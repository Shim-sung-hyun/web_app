[공통]
Draw ONE animal only, in exactly the same style as the attached reference sheet: cute but not babyish flat cartoon, clean dark-brown outlines, flat natural colours with soft simple shading, friendly face with small round eyes.
The animal is drawn WHOLE (head to tail, all legs), seen from the SIDE (head clearly to the left or to the right), and it fills almost the whole square image (a small white margin only).
Because the picture is large, keep the outline THIN and even (about the thickness of a pencil line, not a marker) and draw clean crisp details.
NO background, NO ground, NO water, NO plants, NO rocks, NO shadow, NO text, NO frame — only the animal on PURE WHITE. Square image.
